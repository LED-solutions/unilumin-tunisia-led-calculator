# Changelog

## v103 — 2026-11-13
- LST: choose the brightness (LST4.44: 5,500 / 6,000 / 6,500 cd/m²; LST6, LST8, LST10: 6,000 to 10,000 cd/m²). It shows in the specs and the PDFs.
- LST power always uses the minimum of the datasheet range: LST4.44 650 / 213 W/m², LST6 640 / 213 W/m², LST8 and LST10 600 / 200 W/m² (max / typical).

## v102 — 2026-11-12
- Sale: new cabinet Unilumin ULW III 1.2 Pro (indoor, P1.25, 16:9 cabinet 600 × 337.5 × 47.8 mm, 480 × 270 px, 800 cd/m², 125 W max / 41 W typical per cabinet, 5 kg, 7680 Hz). 8 × 8 cabinets give a 4.8 × 2.7 m 4K screen. Also in the LED totem.
- Export PDFs: much more contrast in the receiving card, power supply and power cabling drawings: darker module and cabinet outlines, thicker block borders and chains, stronger colours, so they stay visible when printed.

## v101 — 2026-11-11
- Cabinets: the power cabling view now looks like the receiving cards view. Each cabinet is coloured by phase (L1, L2, L3), numbered by circuit (1.1, 1.2…), with the IN point and a dashed IN/OUT chain per 16 A circuit, a legend per circuit, and the 2 cases (single-phase and three-phase) side by side with current, breaker and main cable.

## v100 — 2026-11-11
- Sale: removed the screen orientation and plan view.
- Sale: a place can now have several screens. "+ Add another place" in Screens and places, "+ Add another screen" at the top of Screen size: each screen has its own name, product and size.
- Cabinets (power supply and adapted receiving card inside): no more card load or PSU layout. New "Power cabling" view with the IN/OUT power chains on 16 A circuits, in 2 cases: single-phase entry (cable section and breaker, not recommended for big screens) and three-phase entry with the recommended cable and the L1/L2/L3 split. Data cabling shows the RJ45 chain per port. Also in the Export and Technical PDFs.

## v99 — 2026-11-11
- Sale: the LST cabinet is now the full LST series: LST4.44, LST6, LST8 and LST10, each in 960 × 960, 960 × 640, 1440 × 960 and 1440 × 640 mm (Unilumin LST series datasheet).
- Sale: new look for the screen view: light wall and floor, soft glow of the screen, a better person for scale with a shadow.
- Sale: the modules wide / high fields are now right under Meters / Modules, with the size result under them.
- Sale: "Places" is now "Screens and places", with "+ Add another screen": each screen has its own size and product.

## v98 — 2026-11-06
- Sale: new cabinet Unilumin LST6 (outdoor, P6.67, 6,000 cd/m², 640 W/m² max, IP66 front and rear) in 4 sizes: 960 × 960, 960 × 640, 1440 × 960 and 1440 × 640 mm. Also in the LED totem.
- Sale: screens next to each other. "+ Add a screen next to this one" starts a new screen at one end of the screen before. Each screen has where it faces (north, east… or any angle) and which end it starts from. A plan view (top view) shows the screens, where they face and the angle between them, on the page and in the PDFs.

## v97 — 2026-11-06
- LED totem: Neutrik powerCON (16 A) power inlet that feeds everything inside, from the LED to the player. Above 16 A it switches to an industrial CEE 32 A inlet (three-phase when needed), with a warning.
- RJ45 socket so the player goes online by cable, and a USB socket for the screen configuration cable. Both on the back panel, in the drawings, the bill of materials and the PDFs.

## v96 — 2026-11-06

- It shows the 3D views front and back, the front, side and back drawings with dimensions, power, PSUs, receiving cards, weight and casters, the bill of materials, and checks (socket, stability, casters, player size). Client PDF and Export PDF, with the resolutions page.
